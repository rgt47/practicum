## Why the prose license changed

Through 2026-09-07 this book was licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. That was an
error rather than a policy. The ND term forbids distributing modified
material, and revision and remix are the affordances that define an
open educational resource, so a book under ND does not qualify as one
and cannot be listed in the catalogs through which adoption happens.
The NC term compounded the problem by blocking the print-on-demand
edition and by introducing compatibility conflicts with other openly
licensed material.

The change was made on 2026-09-07 by the sole copyright holder. As
copyright holder, the author retains the ability to grant separate
terms to specific parties; CC BY is the public default, not a
relinquishment of that ability.
